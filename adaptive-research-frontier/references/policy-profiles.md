# Research Policy Profiles

Apply one planner-selected profile. Profiles alter branch allocation, pruning, verification, and stopping; they do not change access permissions or replace `deep-researcher`.

## General

- Balance exploration breadth, evidence quality, and cost.
- Prune clearly irrelevant or duplicate branches early.
- Preserve material contradictions.
- Hand off when the problem map is stable and remaining uncertainty is explicit.

## Exhaustive

- Favor coverage across independent answer families before deepening one branch.
- Track candidate-set completeness and negative evidence.
- Prune only with an explicit exclusion reason.
- Require the handoff to identify likely missing categories and a planned completeness check.
- Keep persistent benchmark memory disabled to avoid contamination.

## Legal

- Preserve jurisdiction, date, procedural posture, authority hierarchy, and citator/applicability needs in every material receipt.
- Use adaptive discovery for issue spotting, factual mapping, competing interpretations, and candidate authorities.
- Prune conservatively when a branch may contain adverse authority, an exception, a limitation, or a procedural bar.
- Require `deep-researcher` to own authority synthesis and material verification after handoff.
- Require the planned phase to run one bounded completeness sweep for directly operative duties, adverse authority, procedural posture, effective dates, and regulated-party/applicability allocation before answering. Reuse accepted receipts; this is a gap check, not a full re-search.
- Keep matter data scoped to the run and planner-approved artifacts.

## Medical

- Preserve population, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, setting, date, evidence level, contraindication, and applicability when relevant.
- Use adaptive discovery for evidence mapping, guideline discovery, competing hypotheses, rare considerations, and evidence gaps.
- Do not prune a branch solely because it is rare when it may contain a contraindication, interaction, adverse effect, or safety-critical alternative.
- Prefer established evidence hierarchies and require planned synthesis plus appropriate human clinical review for patient-specific or safety-sensitive conclusions.
- Require the planned phase to run one bounded completeness sweep for contraindications, material harms, population/applicability limits, evidence quality, and current guideline or regulatory status before answering. Reuse accepted receipts; do not repeat the discovery frontier.
- Keep patient data scoped to approved systems and artifacts; do not persist it as skill memory.

## Custom Constraints

When the ticket supplies stricter domain constraints, apply them on top of the closest profile. Do not weaken a profile or invent a new source hierarchy. If constraints conflict, stop and return to the planner.
